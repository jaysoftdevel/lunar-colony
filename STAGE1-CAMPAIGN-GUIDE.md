## v0.50.15 — Campaign funding and progression

The Contract Office includes 31 one-time customer jobs and a separate, one-time lunar cargo sponsorship. Sign the early research-free jobs first; expand into payload materials, propulsion, avionics, rovers, power and life support as the technology tree unlocks further customers. Technical project completion grants the final payout and discounts linked future research. Advanced jobs finance large launch hardware. Customer reports never reopen after submission.

**Funding audit:** Basic commercial earnings alone are **EUR 94.35M**. All 16 undiscounted technologies total **EUR 21.85M** and the most expensive rocket + lander cost **EUR 37M**. The Contract Office calculates minimum cargo cost from Stage-2 construction recipes; the budget is designed to cover this too before launch. First-delivery premiums, investor awards, and research discounts add flexibility but are not necessary for solvency. The lunar cargo sponsorship's final payout is intentionally excluded because it arrives at launch.

## v0.50.13 — More direct controls

- **Contracts:** click a project to see the brief; double-click to sign (if available) or submit its completed report. A completed job never reopens. Its main action button does exactly the same thing.
- **Tech Tree:** click a research node to inspect it; double-click to buy and unlock it if you can afford it and have prerequisites. The inspector's unlock button remains available.
- **Mission Prep:** click the current timeline step to open its station, or use **GO TO NEXT FACILITY**. Financial confirmation, cargo freeze, and launch remain deliberate facility actions.
- The active bottom tab is highlighted. The **NEXT STEP** recommendation navigates but never spends cash or launches by itself.

## v0.50.12 — Mission and contract persistence

Each customer contract is a **single assignment**: sign once, receive an advance, complete the work, submit once, collect the final payment, and keep the research benefit permanently. Green = completed/closed, gold = ready to deliver, blue = active, cyan = available, gray = locked. Do not expect completed customer jobs to reset. Spend the finite project budget carefully and use the lunar delivery contract to help fund launch hardware.

Leaving Mission Prep never resets the mission. Return via the Mission Prep tab or **Resume Mission** in the Contract Office. On the mission overview, click **Continue at Required Facility** to pick up where you stopped, or select the current gate. The chosen rocket, lander, cargo quantities and completed preparation steps are stored with the campaign save.

# Stage 1 — Career guide (v0.50.11)

**Goal:** fund a real robotic lunar cargo mission, unlocking the technologies needed to build and operate the Moon base.

## The gameplay loop
1. Start Career and open the Program & Contracts Office.
2. Sign a customer contract. The signing advance is credited immediately.
3. Click **ADVANCE TO NEXT REPORT** to pass exactly the amount of planning time until the first active customer report is finished. The game pauses so you can submit it.
4. Click **SUBMIT REPORT / CLAIM PAYMENT** to receive the customer's final payment.
5. Spend earnings on available technology at Research & Engineering.
6. Repeat with new customers, then complete the 10 facility-owned gates. **LAUNCH & DEPLOY TO MOON** starts Stage 2 with precisely the supplies purchased in your cargo manifest.

The **NEXT STEP** shortcut appears in the bottom bar and on the 3D site. It opens the recommended view and selects the useful project, research topic or active mission facility. It does not accept a contract, spend money or launch automatically.

## Why deliver distinct contracts?
- Each technical project supplies evidence, discounting its linked research technology by **20%**, up to **35%** from multiple distinct reports.
- The first delivery of customer projects 2–11 earns an additional **20% of the final payout**. This is listed in the Project Brief before accepting.
- Delivering to **3**, **6**, and **9** distinct customers attracts one-time investor milestone awards of **EUR 750,000**, **EUR 1.5M**, and **EUR 3M**. These are derived from saved completion history and never awarded twice.
- Repeating commercial work yields standard payments but no duplicate first-time bonuses. Additional one-time contracts across each research tier protect against funding deadlocks.

## Useful controls
- **Contract list:** Select a project, inspect its deliverable, signing advance, final payment, tech benefit, and whether it is available.
- **SUBMIT REPORT:** Collect the earned payment when work reaches completion.
- **ADVANCE TO NEXT REPORT:** Jump forward to the next active customer delivery without holding 20X speed or overshooting.
- **NEXT STEP:** Context-aware navigation only, never a financial or mission commitment.
- **Technology:** Selecting a node shows prerequisites, effective cost and capability. If you lack funds, use **EARN FUNDS - CONTRACTS**.
- **Mission Preparation:** Follow Research -> Design -> Test -> Refine -> Plan -> Confirm/Fund -> Build -> Cargo Integration -> Final Confirm -> Launch. The owning facility is shown at each gate.

The first Mission Architecture Study retains its normal EUR 250K advance, EUR 550K delivery payment and 24-day tutorial duration. Its Systems Engineering research evidence now reduces the EUR 400K technology purchase to EUR 320K.

**Mission ownership:** Each lunar mission is one-time only. Launch automatically opens a new Stage-2 robotic base with the selected iron/copper/silicon/plastic/aluminum/water/food/medical stock as real construction inventory and physical depot pallets. All commercial contracts and the lunar mission are one-time only.

**Compatibility:** version 0.50.11 reuses save schema 29, keeps all 12 contract IDs and 16 technologies, and migrates previously completed Stage-1 saves to Stage 2. Independent Stage-2 Free Play and Stage 3 remain unchanged.
